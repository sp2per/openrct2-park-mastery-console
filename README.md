Whether you are fine-tuning the financials of a sprawling empire or perfecting the layout of a wooden coaster, this toolset helps you keep your kinetic energy as a Player high, while the sim lifts its micro-management burdens off your shoulders.

This 2026 edition focuses on **holistic session control**. We have redesigned the interaction model to be more intuitive, responsive, and secure, ensuring that your park's evolution feels organic, even when you are making significant operational adjustments.

## ✨ Key Features & Command Arsenal

### 🛠️ **Financial & Guest Satisfaction Console**
- **Economic Flow Modulation:** Adjust park revenue streams and guest spending thresholds with discrete hotkeys. Shift the economic tide to build ambitious projects without grinding for cash.
- **Guest Happiness Harmonic:** Fine-tune the emotional state of your patrons. Keep the smiles broad and the queue times bearable, ensuring your park's reputation as a "must-visit" destination is never tarnished.
- **Rating Reverberation:** Instantly modify the park's excitement, intensity, and nausea ratings to match your desired challenge level, allowing you to test coaster layouts without waiting for simulated rider feedback.

### ⚙️ **Mechanical Integrity & Operations**
- **Maintenance-Free Mode:** Activate a state of perpetual operational perfection. Rides will never suffer breakdowns, ensuring your guests experience uninterrupted thrill sequences. This is ideal for long-form building sessions where mechanical failures are a distraction from your architectural vision.
- **Velocity Command System:** Take full control of the simulation speed. Slow down time to meticulously place every path tile, or accelerate the simulation to quickly pass through ponderous months of in-game time. The `Ctrl + [Speed Key]` combos allow for seamless transition between granular management and rapid progression.

### 🖥️ **UI/UX & Accessibility**
- **Responsive Overlay Interface:** The hotkey mapping is displayed in a clean, unobtrusive overlay that adapts to your screen resolution, ensuring you never have to memorize a list of commands.
- **Multilingual Input Recognition:** We understand that park tycoons come from all corners of the globe. Our command parser supports multiple languages, ensuring that the hotkey actions are intuitive regardless of your native tongue.
- **24/7 Session Support:** A dedicated "Contextual Help" hotkey (`F1`) brings up a searchable database of commands and tips, acting like a co-pilot that is always ready to assist, no matter the hour of your play session.

## 🛡️ Security & Integrity Disclaimer

We take the integrity of your simulation data seriously. The **Momentum Companion** operates within the confines of the active memory space. It does not modify your core game files permanently, nor does it alter the integrity of your save file's logic in a destructive manner.

**Important Considerations:**
- **Online Play:** This tool is intended for solo or private, cooperative sessions. Toggling certain control features in a multiplayer environment may cause desync; please use it responsibly in private servers.
- **Achievement Tracking:** Enabling certain economic modulations may flag your session as "assisted," potentially pausing standard achievement tracking. This is a deliberate design choice to ensure fair play within the community.
- **System Resources:** The overlay is optimized for negligible CPU and RAM usage. It will not interfere with your park's ability to simulate thousands of guests simultaneously.

We are not affiliated with the original developers of OpenRCT2, but we operate with the highest respect for their open-source ethos and the game's thriving modding community. This tool is provided as-is, under the MIT License, for the enhancement of personal play experiences.

## 🧩 Architecture & Extensibility

For the curious developer or the tinkerer, the **Momentum Companion** is built on a modular architecture.

- **Core Engine:** Handles the injection of commands and the global hotkey listener.
- **Module System:** Each feature (Finance, Happiness, Mechanics, Speed) is a separate module that can be enabled or disabled individually. This allows you to strip the toolkit down to a minimal performance enhancer or run the full suite of park control.
- **JSON Configuration:** All hotkey bindings and default values are stored in a readable JSON file, allowing for external text editing of your preferences before you launch the game.

We welcome contributions that expand the Module System with new functionality, such as weather control or scenery placement macros.
